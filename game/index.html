How to play

  • 1. Pick an order. Click a ticket on the order board to select it. The dots show its recipe; filled dots are already on your plate.
  • 2. Cook what needs cooking. Click a patty, meat or pasta bin and it goes to the grill or pot. Click it again when the ring turns gold (READY) — leave it and it burns.
  • 3. Build the plate. Click ingredient bins to stack them. Click a layer on the plate to remove just that one; CLEAR empties it.
  • 4. Serve. Hit SERVE (or Enter) when the plate matches the ticket exactly. Fast serves and streaks pay more.
  • Watch the clock. A ticket that runs out of patience walks out: −100 points and a lost star. Three stars gone ends the shift.
